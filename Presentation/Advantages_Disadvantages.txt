# Advantages and Disadvantages

## Slide 1: Advantages of Proposed System
*   **Optimized Resource Utilization**: Increases vehicle occupancy, reducing per-capita fuel consumption and carbon footprint.
*   **Economic Efficiency**: Automated fare-splitting and shared costs significantly lower daily commute expenses for riders.
*   **Enhanced Security**: Mandatory identity verification (Firebase/JWT) and community reviews foster a trust-based ecosystem.
*   **Convenience & Connectivity**: "Virtual Grid" logic and logic-based routing provide seamless last-mile connectivity and flexible booking options.
*   **Time Saving**: Real-time matching reduces wait times compared to public transport and eliminates manual coordination delays.

## Slide 2: Disadvantages & Limitations
*   **Internet Dependency**: The platform requires a stable internet connection for real-time tracking and booking, limiting access in remote areas.
*   **Adoption Barrier**: Initial reluctance to trust strangers may slow down user acquisition despite verification measures.
*   **Smartphone Requirement**: Users without modern smartphones cannot access the app or GPS features.
*   **Route Rigidity**: Drivers may be unwilling to deviate even slightly from their fixed routes, potentially limiting matches for some riders.
