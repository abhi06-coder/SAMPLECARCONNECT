# Scope and Objectives

## Product Scope
The project defines a comprehensive MERN-based carpooling ecosystem featuring:
*   **Geospatial Matching**: Dynamic ride discovery within a 20km radius using MongoDB geospatial queries.
*   **Virtual Grid**: Logic-based seat management for multi-segment journeys.
*   **Trust Layer**: Dual-factor authentication (Firebase/JWT) and community-driven reputation systems.
*   **Financial Hub**: Integrated Razorpay wallet for seamless, cashless transactions and automated refunds.

## Core Objectives
*   To democratize access to efficient, route-specific shared mobility.
*   To eliminate manual coordination friction through automated booking and scheduling.
*   To ensure user safety via rigorous identity verification and peer accountability.
*   To promote environmental sustainability by maximizing vehicle occupancy rates.
